Each column is one token of your prompt. Rows are the model's layers, from the token embeddings at the bottom to the final layer at the top. Each cell shows the token that layer would predict next, shaded by how confident it is.

Readout

How to read this
  • Each column is one piece of your prompt. The model reads text in pieces called tokens, so long words are split, for example "Eiffel" into "Eiff" and "el".
  • Each row is one processing step (layer) of the model. The bottom row is the input, the top row is the model's final answer.
  • A cell shows the word that this layer would predict next if the model stopped there. Darker means more confident.
  • The amber line marks where the model settles: from there up, every layer agrees with the final answer.
  • Shading by KL compares the whole list of guesses, not just the top word: darker means the layer already thinks like the final answer.
  • Hover or tap a cell for its top 5 guesses. Clicking or tapping a cell also follows one word through all layers.
